Animate old photos: four portraits from 1892 to 1925, brought to life

Old studio portraits move well when the prompt asks for small things: a blink, a breath, a head turning toward the lens, a smile that starts at the eyes. Ask for big motion and the face drifts away from the person in the photo.

Below are four public-domain portraits next to the clip each one became, with the exact prompt. Every clip was made in one pass with sound (room tone, a chair creak, a soft laugh). To try it on your own family photo, use animate old photos on Movoa.

Three things that kept the faces right

  1. Start every prompt with "keep the original faces, colours and film look", so the clip does not colourise or smooth the photo.
  2. Name one small action per person. Two people get one action each, not a scene.
  3. Keep the camera still, framed like the original portrait; at most a very slight push-in.
